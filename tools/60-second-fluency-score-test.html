5 Ways to Sound More Fluent

  • 🗣️ Speak in full sentences: Avoid giving one-word answers. Expand on your ideas.
  • ⏸️ Pause, don't fill: Slow down slightly and pause silently instead of filling the silence with "um" or "uh".
  • 🔗 Link your ideas: Use transition words like first, then, and finally to structure your thoughts.
  • 💡 Give reasons and examples: Whenever you make a point, follow it up with a reason ("because...") and an example.
  • 📅 Practise daily: Try one surprise topic every single day to build quick-thinking skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 60-second fluency test work?

It gives you a surprise topic, records you speaking for up to 60 seconds, and analyzes your speech for pace, word count, and filler words to estimate your fluency level.

Is my voice recording stored anywhere?

No. All speech recognition happens directly in your browser. No audio is ever uploaded or saved to our servers.

Which browsers support the microphone recording?

For the best experience, use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari on iOS. If your browser doesn't support the Web Speech API, a typing fallback is provided.

How do XP and streaks work?

You earn 15 XP for every completed fluency test. Practising once a day increases your daily streak. This helps you stay consistent!
