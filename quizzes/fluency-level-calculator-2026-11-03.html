📖 How Fluency Is Really Measured

  • Fluency ≠ perfection. It's speed + naturalness: responding without translating in your head.
  • Collocations win: natives say "rain-check", "tied up", "run it by you" — not textbook translations.
  • Grammar that matters most: tenses, prepositions (in/on/at), and subject-verb agreement cover 80% of daily speech.
  • The 60-second rule: if you can answer small talk, emails, and opinions without pausing to translate, you're intermediate+.
  • Fastest upgrade: shadow native phrases daily — hear it, say it immediately, repeat until automatic.
