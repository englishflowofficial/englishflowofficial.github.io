📖 4 Rules for Clear Workplace Pronunciation

  • Rule 1: Slow down on key vowels — rushing makes English syllables blend awkwardly. Give content words full weight.
  • Rule 2: Link consonant to vowel — say "share it" like "sha-rit" for natural connected speech.
  • Rule 3: Stress the verb and noun — in "schedule a quick call", emphasize sched- and call.
  • Rule 4: Keep an upright posture — breathing from your diaphragm projects immediate voice authority.
