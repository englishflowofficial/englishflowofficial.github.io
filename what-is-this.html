Master Daily Spoken English Vocabulary

Why do English learners say “Cut the banana” or “Open the light”? +

These are direct translation errors from native languages (like Hindi, Spanish, or Chinese). In natural English, specific actions require specific collocations: we peel a banana, turn on a light, and crack an egg.

How does this quiz build my speaking confidence? +

By actively contrasting the common mistake with the natural phrase, your brain replaces clumsy literal translations with native collocations. Audio pronunciation lets you hear and repeat the rhythm of real speech.
