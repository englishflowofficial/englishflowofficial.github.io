📖 The 30-Second Answer Formula

  • Present: 'I'm a [role] with [X] years in [field].' — start with who you are right now.
  • Past: 'Previously, I led [project/achievement] that resulted in [result].' — highlight one key win.
  • Future: 'And I'm looking to bring that experience to help your team with [company goal].' — tie it to them.
  • Keep it punchy: Aim for 30 to 45 seconds total to maintain high engagement.
  • Ditch the life story: Leave out your childhood, university days, and unrelated personal trivia.
  • Speak with active verbs: Use words like 'spearheaded,' 'built,' 'scaled,' and 'delivered.'

    • Flo-tip: Practice your 30-second pitch out loud three times before your next interview!
