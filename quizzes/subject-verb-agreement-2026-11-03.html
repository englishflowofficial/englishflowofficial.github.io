📖 Subject-Verb Agreement Rules

  • He/She/It + verb-s: she works, he watches
  • Do vs Does: they do / she does — "does" only with he/she/it
  • Has vs Have: he has / they have
  • Each / Everyone / Somebody = singular: each student has, everyone knows
