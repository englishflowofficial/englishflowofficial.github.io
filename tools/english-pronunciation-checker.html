Frequently Asked Questions

How does the pronunciation checker work?
It uses your browser's speech recognition to match your spoken words against the target sentence, providing instant word-by-word feedback on what was clearly understood.

Is my voice recording stored anywhere?
No, it is processed entirely on your device in your browser. We do not store or upload your audio.

Which browsers support the live mic recording?
Chrome, Edge, Safari, and other modern browsers supporting the Web Speech API and MediaRecorder. If unsupported, a typed fallback is provided.

How do XP and streak work?
Earn 15 XP for completing a check, plus a 10 XP bonus for 90%+ word accuracy. Practice daily to build your streak!
