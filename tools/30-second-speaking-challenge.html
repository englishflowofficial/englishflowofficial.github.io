📖 5 Rules for Confident Impromptu Speaking

  • 💡
    Pause, Don't Fill. Silence is powerful. Instead of saying "um" or "like", take a deep breath.
  • 🎯
    One Core Idea. Don't try to say everything. Pick one main point and deliver it clearly.
  • 🏗️
    Use the PREP Framework. Point, Reason, Example, Point. It instantly structures your thoughts.
  • 🐢
    Slow Down. When nervous, we rush. Aim for a calm pace (around 120-140 words per minute).
  • 😊
    Smile While Speaking. It naturally warms your tone and tricks your brain into feeling more relaxed.
🦉 Flo says: "Perfection is the enemy of fluency. Focus on connection, not correction!"
