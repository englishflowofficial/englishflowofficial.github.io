FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about English Flow

Learn spoken English comfortably with zero pressure, daily streaks, and bite-sized quizzes.

How can I practice speaking English daily for free? +

English Flow provides 30 daily bite-sized speaking lessons with real-life conversational phrases, pronunciation shadowing, sentence building puzzles, and instant feedback. No login or subscription is required.

Is English Flow similar to Duolingo for spoken English? +

Yes! English Flow incorporates gamified, high-dopamine learning mechanics: our mascot Flo, daily streaks, XP points, tactile 3D interactive quiz cards, and cheerful sound effects to make daily speaking practice exciting and addictive.

How do I master all 12 English tenses easily? +

Our dedicated Tenses room breaks down all 12 English tenses (Present, Past, and Future simple, continuous, perfect, and perfect continuous) into simple formulas, everyday examples, and 72 interactive practice questions.

Can I download English speaking worksheets as a PDF? +

Yes! You can view and print our comprehensive 30-Day Speaking Workbook PDF directly from the Worksheets page to practice offline anytime.
