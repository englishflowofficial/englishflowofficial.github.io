📖 5 Textbook → Natural Upgrades

  • ☕ I want coffee → Can I get a latte to go? — soften every request.
  • 📧 Do the needful / revert back → Could you please look into this?
  • 💬 What is your good name? → What do you do?
  • 🤝 You are wrong → I see it a bit differently… — disagree without burning bridges.
  • 📞 Cutting the phone → Let's catch up soon!
