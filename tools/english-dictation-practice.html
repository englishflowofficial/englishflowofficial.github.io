    📖 5 Dictation Tips

    • Listen twice before typing: Catch the full meaning on the first play, then focus on exact wording on the second.
    • Type the small words too: Tiny unstressed words like a, the, to, and of carry most dictation errors.
    • Replay the tricky sentence: Replays are unlimited by design — drop to 0.85x Slow to hear linked sounds clearly.
    • Read your diff out loud: Speaking the corrected sentence once locks the spelling and sound together.
    • Practise one pack a day: Five focused sentences daily beat an occasional marathon session.
    🦉 Flo's tip: Watch out for apostrophes in contractions like they're, it's, and we've — your ears hear one smooth syllable, but your keyboard needs the hidden word!

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does English Dictation Practice work?

    Pick one of four 5-sentence packs (or Shuffle All). Your browser reads each hidden sentence aloud at 0.85x, 1.0x, or 1.15x speed. Type what you hear and click Check My Spelling for an instant word-by-word diff showing matched, misspelled, missed, and extra words, plus a 1-line listening note.

    Is the audio a real human voice?

    No. Audio is generated on your device using native-style browser audio (Web Speech speechSynthesis in en-US), not a studio human recording. This lets you replay unlimited times for free with no sign-up.

    Do I need a microphone to practise?

    No. The full dictation round works with typing alone. Your microphone is only used if you tap the optional Say It Back (Mic) bonus after checking a sentence.

    How do XP and daily streaks work?

    Every completed 5-sentence round earns +20 XP. Score 90%+ word accuracy with 2 or fewer replays for +10 bonus XP, and speak all 5 sentences back at 80%+ for an optional +10 mic-only bonus. Streak and XP are stored locally in your browser.
