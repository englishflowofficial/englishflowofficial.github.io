    5 IELTS Part 1 Tips

    • Answer in 2–3 sentences: Aim for roughly 25–35 words so your reply feels natural and developed without rambling.
    • Always add a reason: Turn a flat "Yes, I do" into "Yes, I do, mostly because…" to show natural linking.
    • Give one short example: Anchor your reason with a quick real-life detail from your week or routine.
    • Don't memorise answers: Memorised scripts sound stiff and break down if the examiner asks a small follow-up.
    • Keep talking until the timer ends: Use the full 20-second rhythm to build comfort speaking without freezing.
    🦉 Flo's tip: If you catch yourself saying "um" or "actually", pause for half a beat, smile, and jump straight into your example!

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does IELTS Speaking Part 1 practice work?

    Choose a 4-question topic pack (or Shuffle All). A browser voice asks each question aloud, a 20-second countdown runs while you speak or type, and you get instant word-count, filler-word, and development feedback plus audio playback.

    Is the examiner a real person?

    No. Questions are spoken using your device's browser voice (speechSynthesis in en-US), not a human examiner or studio recording. All feedback is a practice estimate and development check only — never an official IELTS band score.

    Do I need a microphone?

    No. A microphone enables live speech recognition, a volume meter, and audio playback, but if mic access is denied or unsupported you can use Typed Answer Mode with the same 20-second timer and feedback.

    How do XP and daily streaks work?

    Completing a 4-question round awards +20 XP, with a +10 XP bonus when all 4 answers reach the 25+ word sweet spot. Your XP and daily streak are stored locally in your browser.
