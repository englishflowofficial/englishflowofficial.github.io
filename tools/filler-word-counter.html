📖 5 Ways to Cut Filler Words

  • 1. Pause instead of "um" — embrace silence. A 1-second pause sounds confident, while filler words signal hesitation.
  • 2. Slow down your speaking tempo — give your brain a 2-second buffer to construct sentences before speaking.
  • 3. Plan your first sentence — know exactly how you will open so you start with zero filler momentum.
  • 4. Practise with a timer — regular 60-second sprints build subconscious muscle memory against fillers.
  • 5. Record and replay yourself — listening back to your voice makes you instantly aware of hidden filler habits.

🦉 Flo tip: When you feel an "actually" or "like" coming, close your mouth and inhale through your nose. Silence is power!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Filler Word Counter detect and count filler words?

The tool uses your browser's Web Speech API to transcribe your speech live, pattern-matching against common conversational fillers: 'um', 'uh', 'like', 'basically', 'actually', 'you know', and 'erm'. Each instance is highlighted on screen and tallied in real time.

Which web browsers support microphone speech recognition?

Live microphone speech recognition works seamlessly in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser or device lacks microphone recognition support, you can use the built-in 60-second typed mode.

Is my voice recording stored or processed on external servers?

No. Speech transcription and filler counting occur entirely within your local browser session. English Flow does not record, store, or transmit your audio to remote databases.

How do streaks and XP work in the Filler Word Counter?

You earn +15 XP for completing a 60-second speech session, plus an extra +10 XP mastery bonus if you keep your fillers to 0 or 1. Completing your first challenge of the day advances your daily English Flow streak.
