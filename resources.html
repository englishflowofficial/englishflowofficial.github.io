12 English Tenses Simplified

Everyday formulas and high-frequency conversation examples.

📺 Companion Video Lesson

Present Simple

Habits, daily routines, general facts.

"I drink coffee every morning."

Past Simple

Finished action at a specific time in the past.

"I went to the store yesterday."

Present Perfect

Life experiences or past actions with present result.

"I have lived here for 5 years."

Future Simple

Spontaneous decisions, predictions, promises.

"I will call you in five minutes."
