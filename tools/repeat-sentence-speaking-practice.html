📖 5 Repeat Sentence Tips

  • 1. Listen for meaning in chunks, not word by word — picture the scene in your head and group the sentence into 2 or 3 meaningful phrases.
  • 2. Start speaking right after the chime — waiting too long lets the final words fade from your short-term working memory.
  • 3. Say the part you remember confidently — partial credit applies — even if you forget two words in the middle, deliver the opening and closing chunks smoothly.
  • 4. Copy the speaker's stress and rhythm — mimicking the melody of the sentence helps your brain recall the small connecting words automatically.
  • 5. Never stay silent — repeating 50% of the words in sequence earns solid partial credit, whereas silence scores zero.

🦉 Flo tip: Anchor the very first 3 words and the very last 2 words in your mind as you listen — your brain will bridge the middle naturally!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Repeat Sentence speaking practice work?

You pick a difficulty tier (Short 7–9 words, Medium 10–12 words, or Long 13–14 words) and tap Hear Sentence. The sentence is spoken once using native-style browser speech while the text stays hidden. After a short chime cue, you have 15 seconds to repeat the sentence from memory into your microphone, and then the hidden sentence reveals word-by-word showing which words were recognised in sequence.

Is my voice audio stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and browser speech recognition transcript stay 100% inside your local web browser session. Nothing is uploaded, stored on external servers, or shared.

Which web browsers support microphone recording and speech recognition?

Live microphone speech recognition and audio recording work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support Web Speech recognition, you can still listen to the hidden sentence and type what you remember in typed fallback mode.

How do XP and daily streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +20 XP for completing a repeat-sentence check, plus a +10 XP bonus when 90% or more of the target words are recognised in sequence from memory using your microphone. Completing your first session of the day advances your daily English Flow streak.
