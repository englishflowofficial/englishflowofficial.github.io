Frequently Asked Questions

How does the English conversation simulator work?

You choose one of six everyday or workplace scenarios and start a 4-turn practice call with a scripted practice partner. The browser speaks each partner line aloud, you reply using your microphone, and at the end you receive a fluency summary with speaking pace, filler count, and playback of your turns.

Is my voice recording stored anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and call transcript stay strictly inside your local browser session. Nothing is uploaded, saved on external servers, or shared.

Which browsers support the live microphone?

Live microphone speech recognition and audio recording work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support speech recognition, you can still hear the scripted partner and type your replies in typed call mode.

How do XP and streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +25 XP for completing a 4-turn practice call, plus a +10 XP bonus when all 4 turns are completed using your microphone. Completing your first call of the day advances your daily English Flow streak.
