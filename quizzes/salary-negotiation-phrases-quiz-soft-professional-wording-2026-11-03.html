📖 Quick Study Cheat-Sheet: Professional Salary Negotiation

  • Rule 1: Frame around value, not personal need — anchor your request to market research and business impact.
  • Rule 2: Use soft modal verbs — phrases like 'was hoping,' 'would love to,' and 'could we explore' prevent pushiness.
  • Rule 3: Tie requests to measurable deliverables — quantify your wins and expanded responsibilities.
  • Rule 4: Keep the dialogue collaborative — use phrases like 'bridge the gap' and focus on long-term team success.

Flo-tip: Practice these soft negotiation phrases out loud before scheduling your compensation review!
