📖 Quick Study Cheat-Sheet: Strong Speech Openings

  • Rule 1: Paint a Vivid Picture — use scenario openers like 'Picture this...' to trigger audience imagination immediately.
  • Rule 2: Acknowledge the Elephant in the Room — tackle tough realities with relatable, idiomatic business language.
  • Rule 3: Frame Immediate Value — state the direct outcome or benefit to your listeners in your very first sentence.
  • Rule 4: Ditch Lengthy Preliminaries — skip slide housekeeping and jump straight into compelling insights and results.
  • Rule 5: Use Dynamic Bridges — keep momentum moving forward with phrases like 'That brings us directly to today's core focus.'

Flo-tip: Deliver your first sentence without glancing at your notes to project total confidence!
