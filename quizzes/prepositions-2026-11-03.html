📖 Prepositions, Finally Clear

  • AT — exact times & points: at 7pm, at the airport, at night
  • ON — days & surfaces: on Monday, on the table, on time
  • IN — months, years, enclosed spaces: in July, in 2026, in the box
  • TO — direction: go to work · FOR — purpose/receiver: a gift for you
