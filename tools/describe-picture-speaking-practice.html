📖 5 Picture Description Tips

  • 1. Start with “This picture shows…” and name the scene in one line — anchor your listener immediately with the setting and time of day.
  • 2. Name the biggest thing first, then two details — describe the central action before moving outward to smaller objects.
  • 3. Use place words — on the left, in the background, in the foreground — spatial signposts give your description clear structure and flow.
  • 4. Keep talking until the timer ends, don't stop at 20 seconds — aim for the full 30–40 second sweet spot by adding colours, actions, and an overall summary.
  • 5. Record and replay yourself and count how many key things you named — checking your recording against the 5-item checklist trains your eye to scan faster.

🦉 Flo tip: During your 25-second study window, point your finger at 5 distinct objects on the screen so you never run out of things to say!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Describe a Picture speaking practice work?

You pick one of eight illustrated practice pictures, study the scene and its five key elements for 25 seconds, and then describe the picture out loud for 40 seconds into your microphone — timed like the real PTE Describe Image task. Afterwards, we check your transcript for key elements named, words recognised, speaking pace in WPM, filler words, and 2s+ pauses.

Is my voice audio stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and browser speech recognition transcript stay strictly inside your local browser session. Nothing is uploaded, stored on external servers, or shared.

Which web browsers support microphone recording and speech recognition?

Live microphone speech recognition and audio recording work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support Web Speech recognition, you can still use the 25s study timer, 40s speaking timer, and typed fallback mode.

How do XP and daily streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +25 XP for completing a picture description, plus a +10 XP bonus when you name at least 4 of the 5 key elements and speak for 30 to 40 seconds. Completing your first session of the day advances your daily English Flow streak.
