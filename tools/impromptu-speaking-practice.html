        📖 5 Impromptu Speaking Tips

        • 1. Use PREP — point, reason, example, point — state your position first, give one clear reason, share a real story, and restate your point at the end.
        • 2. Buy thinking time with one opening line — start calmly with "That is an important question, and my main point is…" while your brain organises the middle.
        • 3. Give one specific example, not five vague ones — a single 30-second personal story is far more convincing and easier to deliver than a list of abstract ideas.
        • 4. Keep talking until the timer enters the 1:00–2:00 sweet spot — when the traffic light turns yellow at 1:00, transition into your closing point.
        • 5. Record and replay yourself — listening back to your own 2-minute take is the fastest way to catch filler words and rushed transitions.

        🦉 Flo tip: You don't need a perfect opinion on a surprise topic — pick one side within 5 seconds and commit to one real-life example!

        Frequently Asked Questions

        How does impromptu speaking practice work on this page?

        You pick a topic category (or draw any surprise topic), listen to the prompt via native-style browser audio, take 30 seconds to jot an opening line and one example using the PREP framework, and then speak for 1 to 2 minutes against a traffic-light timer. At the end, you see your words recognised, speaking pace in WPM, filler count, PREP signposts found, and a playback player for your own recording.

        Is my voice audio stored or uploaded anywhere?

        No. Your voice recording and browser speech recognition transcript stay strictly inside your local browser session. Nothing is uploaded, stored on external servers, or shared.

        Which browsers support microphone recording and live speech recognition?

        Live microphone speech recognition and audio recording work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support Web Speech recognition, you can still use the topic generator, 30s prep timer, traffic-light clock, and typed fallback mode.

        How do XP and daily streaks work on English Flow?

        You earn +25 XP for completing an impromptu speaking round, plus a +10 XP bonus when you use all 4 PREP signposts or speak for 60–120 seconds with under 3% filler words. Completing your first practice session of the day increments your daily English Flow streak.
