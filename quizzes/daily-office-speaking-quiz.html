📖 Office English Upgrades

  • ❌ Please revert back → ✅ Please reply / get back to me
  • ❌ As per your mail → ✅ As mentioned in your email
  • ❌ Do the needful → ✅ Please take care of this
  • ✅ Could you please share an update? — polite status check
  • ✅ Let's circle back tomorrow. — natural for "talk later"
  • ✅ Thanks for flagging this. — professional acknowledgement
