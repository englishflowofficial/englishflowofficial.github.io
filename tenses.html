1. Present Tenses

Simple Present
Subj + V1(s/es)
"I study English every day."
Habits, routines, general facts.
Present Continuous
Subj + am/is/are + V-ing
"I am studying right now."
Actions happening exactly at this moment.
Present Perfect
Subj + have/has + V3
"I have studied English for 3 years."
Actions started in the past that continue or have a result in the present.
Present Perfect Continuous
Subj + have/has been + V-ing
"I have been studying for 2 hours."
Emphasizes the duration of a continuous action started in the past.

2. Past Tenses

Simple Past
Subj + V2
"I studied yesterday."
Completed actions at a specific time in the past.
Past Continuous
Subj + was/were + V-ing
"I was studying when you called."
Actions in progress at a specific time in the past, often interrupted.
Past Perfect
Subj + had + V3
"I had already studied before dinner."
An action that happened before another action in the past.
Past Perfect Continuous
Subj + had been + V-ing
"I had been studying for an hour when it rained."
Duration of a past action up to a certain point in the past.

3. Future Tenses

Simple Future
Subj + will + V1
"I will study tomorrow."
Predictions, promises, or sudden decisions.
Future Continuous
Subj + will be + V-ing
"I will be studying at 8 PM."
Actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.
Future Perfect
Subj + will have + V3
"I will have finished studying by 10 PM."
Actions that will be completed before a certain time in the future.
Future Perfect Continuous
Subj + will have been + V-ing
"I will have been studying for 3 hours by midnight."
Duration of an action leading up to a specific time in the future.
