📖 Conditionals in 30 Seconds

  • Zero — facts: If you heat ice, it melts. (present + present)
  • First — real future: If it rains, I will stay. (present + will)
  • Second — imaginary now: If I were rich, I would travel. (past + would)
  • Third — regret: If I had studied, I would have passed. (past perfect + would have)
