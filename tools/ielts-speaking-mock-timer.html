    💡 5 IELTS Speaking Tips

    • Plan efficiently: Use the 1-minute prep time to note down exactly 3 to 4 keywords, not full sentences.
    • Keep talking: The examiner will stop you when the 2 minutes are up. Don't worry if you get cut off—it means you had plenty to say!
    • Use signposting: Link your ideas smoothly with words like first, next, and finally to improve coherence.
    • Expand answers: Give a clear reason and a specific example for every bullet point on the cue card.
    • Review yourself: Record and replay your answers. Listen for awkward pauses, filler words, or repeated vocabulary.

    Flo says: Consistency is key! Do one mock test every day.
