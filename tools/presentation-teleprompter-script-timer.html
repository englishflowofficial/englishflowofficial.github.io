📖 5 Ways to Sound Confident on Stage

  • 1. Rehearse at your real pace, not your fast nervous pace — when adrenaline strikes, speakers accelerate without realizing it. Lock in your calm cadence here first.
  • 2. Pause at commas and full stops — commas give listeners a micro-second to process; full stops mark authority.
  • 3. Look up at the end of each sentence, not at the screen — glance at the teleprompter for the start of the thought, then deliver the final words directly to your audience.
  • 4. Mark 3 words to stress in every paragraph — highlighting key nouns or action verbs prevents a robotic, flat delivery.
  • 5. Time your talk twice — once slow, once at full pace — knowing your exact timing boundaries eliminates clock anxiety entirely.

🦉 Flo tip: A 2-second pause after an important point creates suspense and emphasizes your leadership. Never rush into your next slide!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the teleprompter timing work?

The teleprompter counts the total words in your speech script and calculates the exact duration required to read it at your chosen words-per-minute (WPM) pace. As you speak, it smoothly scrolls the script text across your screen at that precise pace.

What speaking pace should I pick for my speech?

For presentations, business pitches, and keynotes, a pace between 120 and 140 WPM is ideal. It allows your audience to easily absorb key takeaways while keeping your delivery natural, confident, and energetic.

Is my speech script stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your script, voice recognition, and timing data remain entirely within your private browser session. English Flow never stores, saves, or transmits your speech texts to remote servers.

How do streaks and XP work on English Flow?

You earn +15 XP for completing a full speech rehearsal, plus an extra +10 XP pacing bonus if you finish within 10 seconds of your target time estimate. Completing your first practice session each day advances your daily streak.
