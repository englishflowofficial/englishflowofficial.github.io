📖 5 Interview Answer Tips

  • 1. Structure every answer with STAR — Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep Situation and Task under 25 seconds so you can highlight your Action.
  • 2. Aim for 60–90 seconds — under 45 seconds sounds shallow and unprepared; over 120 seconds feels rambling and unfocused.
  • 3. End on a result with a number — metrics (e.g. "reduced turnaround time by 30%") make your accomplishments concrete and memorable.
  • 4. Slow down and pause instead of filling silence — when searching for words, close your mouth and take a calm breath instead of leaning on "um" or "like".
  • 5. Record yourself and listen back — hearing your own cadence before the real interview reveals nervous speech habits instantly.

🦉 Flo tip: The strongest candidates don't rush. A confident 2-second pause before delivering your final sentence signals authority and poise!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 90-second interview timer work?

The tool simulates a real interview: you hear a question spoken aloud by a hiring-manager voice, get 5 seconds of think time, then speak for up to 90–120 seconds. Recruiter consensus holds that 60–90 seconds is the ideal sweet spot for interview answers.

Is my voice recording stored anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and transcription stay strictly inside your local web browser session. Nothing is ever uploaded, analyzed on external servers, or stored.

Which browsers support microphone recording and speech recognition?

Microphone recording and live speech transcription work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support Web Speech, an interactive typed fallback mode is automatically enabled.

How do XP and streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +25 XP for completing an interview question rehearsal, plus a +15 XP bonus when your answer lands in the 60–90 second sweet spot with under 3% filler words. Completing your first challenge of the day advances your daily streak.
