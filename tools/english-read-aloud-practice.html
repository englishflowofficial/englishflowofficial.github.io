📖 5 Read-Aloud Tips

  • 1. Read in phrases, not word by word — group 3 to 5 words together into meaningful thought chunks so your voice flows naturally.
  • 2. Breathe at the chunk markers ( / ) — use commas, conjunctions, and full stops as your natural places to pause and take a quick breath.
  • 3. Keep going after a mistake, never stop — if you stumble on a word, do not backtrack or repeat yourself; keep your forward rhythm steady.
  • 4. Stress the key words in each sentence — give nouns, main verbs, and numbers slightly more volume while gliding lightly over small grammar words.
  • 5. Record and replay yourself — listen to your own recording beside the model audio to spot flat intonation or rushed endings.

🦉 Flo tip: During your 35-second prep window, whisper any long names or numbers out loud once so your tongue is ready when the mic opens!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the English Read Aloud test work?

You choose one of eight 55–65 word workplace or academic passages (or paste your own 40–90 word paragraph), skim it during a 35-second prep countdown with optional phrase chunk markers ( / ), and then read it aloud into your microphone within 40 seconds. The tool scores your words recognised vs missed, reading pace in WPM, and 2s+ hesitations.

Is my voice audio stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and browser speech recognition transcript stay strictly inside your local browser session. Nothing is uploaded, stored on external servers, or shared.

Which web browsers support microphone recording and speech recognition?

Live microphone speech recognition and audio recording work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support Web Speech recognition, you can still use the prep timer, listen to the native-style browser audio, and run a typed check.

How do XP and daily streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +20 XP for completing a read-aloud test, plus a +10 XP mastery bonus when you achieve 90% or higher words recognised with zero 2s+ hesitations. Completing your first session of the day advances your daily English Flow streak.
