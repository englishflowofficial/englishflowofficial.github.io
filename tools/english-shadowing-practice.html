📖 How Shadowing Works

  • 1. Listen first — hear the passage once from start to finish so your ear catches where the speaker pauses and stresses key words.
  • 2. Copy the rhythm, not just the words — focus on musical cadence and word linking (like "going to" → "gonna") rather than pronouncing every word like a robot.
  • 3. Shadow half a beat behind the speaker — in Sync Shadow mode, speak right on the heels of the karaoke highlight so you match the natural speed.
  • 4. Loop hard lines at 0.85x — switch to 0.85x Training speed whenever a sentence feels tongue-twisting, then step back up to 1.0x Natural.
  • 5. Record and compare yourself — play back your recording right next to the native-style browser audio to hear if your timing and flow match.

🦉 Flo tip: Don't worry if you miss a word while shadowing in real time — keep moving with the rhythm cursor just like a musician staying on beat!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is English shadowing practice and how does this tool work?

Shadowing is a speaking technique where you listen to a passage and repeat it aloud to match the speaker's rhythm, pace, and connected speech. This tool plays built-in everyday passages using native-style browser audio with karaoke word highlighting, records your voice, and compares your words recognised and timing sync against the target passage.

Do I need a microphone to use the shadowing tool?

Live microphone shadowing and audio playback work best in Google Chrome on desktop and Android. If you do not have a microphone or your browser does not support Web Speech recognition, you can still listen to the karaoke-paced audio, shadow aloud on your own, and use the built-in typed check mode.

Is my voice audio uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Your voice recording and speech recognition transcript stay 100% inside your local web browser session. Nothing is uploaded to any server or saved after you close the page.

How do XP and daily streaks work on English Flow?

You earn +20 XP for completing a shadowing round, plus a +10 XP rhythm bonus when your speaking duration lands within ±15% of the native benchmark duration. Completing your first practice session of the day increments your daily English Flow streak.
