PRESENT · SIMPLE

Present simple

subject + verb (+ s / es)

Habits, routines, schedules and things that are generally true.

“I check my messages every morning.”

−

I do not check my messages at night.

?

Do you check your messages every morning?

subject + was / were + verb-ing

THE ONES THAT TRIP US UP

Two tenses, one real difference.

Most tense mistakes come from three pairs. Read these once and a lot of English gets easier.

Past simple vs present perfect

FINISHED TIME

“I saw that film last night.” — the time is over, so use the past simple.

STILL CONNECTED

“I have seen that film.” — when it happened does not matter, the experience counts now.

Quick test. If you can say yesterday, last week or in 2020, use the past simple.

Present simple vs present continuous

ALWAYS TRUE

“I work in a hospital.” — your usual routine or job.

RIGHT NOW

“I am working from home this week.” — temporary, happening around now.

Quick test. Ask yourself: is this my normal life, or just this moment?

Will vs going to

DECIDED NOW

“The phone is ringing — I will get it.” — a decision made as you speak.

ALREADY PLANNED

“I am going to start a course in May.” — a plan or clear evidence.

Quick test. Look at those clouds — it is going to rain. The evidence is right there.
