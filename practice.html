YOUR 5-DAY CONFIDENCE PLAN

Fifteen minutes can change your flow.

Choose one day, press play and say a few phrases out loud. That is enough.

  1. DAY 01

    Listen for the feeling

    Put on a slow English conversation. Listen once without stopping. Your only job is to notice the general idea.

    TRY: no translating for 5 minutes
    Morning person vs night person ↗
  2. DAY 02

    Shadow one sentence

    Pause after one short sentence. Copy the rhythm, not just the words. Repeat it three times in a relaxed voice.

    TRY: 3 repeats, then move on
    30-day shadowing, day one ↗
  3. DAY 03

    Use a natural reply

    Pick one phrase you could say this week. Put it into a tiny imaginary conversation with a friend or colleague.

    TRY: say it without reading
    Respond naturally in English ↗
  4. DAY 04

    Talk about your life

    Speak for one minute about your routine. Imperfect English is welcome — finishing is more important than fixing every word.

    TRY: record a 60-second voice note
    Talk about your day ↗
  5. DAY 05

    Celebrate your progress

    Repeat your favourite lesson. This time, notice the words you understand and the phrases you can say more easily.

    TRY: choose one win from this week
    Think and speak naturally ↗

THE ENGLISH FLOW METHOD

Listen. Copy. Use.

01

Listen for meaning

You do not need to catch every word. Stay with the story.

02

Copy the rhythm

Repeat short, useful lines until they feel familiar in your mouth.

03

Use it your way

Change the phrase so it matches your own life and voice.
