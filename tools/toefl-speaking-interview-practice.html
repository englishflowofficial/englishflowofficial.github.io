    5 Tips for the 2026 TOEFL Take an Interview Task

    • Start within 2 seconds: With zero prep time, open immediately with a direct answer while your brain lines up reason one.
    • Ride the 4-question arc: Expect Question 1 (personal opinion), Question 2 (real experience), Question 3 (comparison), and Question 4 (future speculation).
    • Aim for 90–130 words per answer: Two clear reasons plus one specific example naturally fill 45 seconds without rushing.
    • Signpost shifts cleanly: Use transitions like first, for example, on the other hand, and therefore so each answer sounds structured.
    • Don't freeze on a small slip: Self-correct once if needed and keep moving — steady flow beats chasing perfection.
    🦉 Flo's tip: Reuse a concrete example from Question 2 when you reach Question 4's future prediction — it sounds natural and saves thinking time!

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the 2026 TOEFL Speaking Take an Interview task?

    In the 2026 TOEFL iBT Speaking section, Take an Interview asks 4 consecutive questions on a single topic with zero preparation time and 45 seconds to answer each question, progressing from opinion and experience to comparison and future outlook.

    Is this an official TOEFL score or real human interviewer?

    No. Questions are spoken using your device's browser voice (speechSynthesis in en-US), and all feedback is a practice estimate — not an official TOEFL score.

    Do I need a microphone, and is my audio stored?

    No. A microphone enables live speech recognition, a volume meter, and local MediaRecorder playback (nothing is uploaded or stored on any server). If mic access is denied or unsupported, sticky 150s typed mode activates automatically.

    How do XP and daily streaks work?

    Completing a 4-question interview round earns +20 XP, plus +10 bonus XP when all 4 answers reach 90 or more words. XP and daily streaks are saved locally in your browser.
