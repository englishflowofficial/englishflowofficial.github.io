Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Daily Speaking Challenge work?

Every UTC day unlocks one fresh everyday speaking topic worldwide. Tap Start, speak for 30 seconds (minimum 15 seconds and 20 words to complete), and get an instant practice estimate out of 100 plus a shareable score card.

Is my audio stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your audio stays in your browser and is never uploaded or stored on any server. Everything runs locally on your device with no account required.

Which browsers support the microphone?

Modern Chrome, Edge, and Safari support live microphone speech recognition and waveform bars. If your browser does not support voice capture or mic access is denied, a typed fallback opens automatically so you can still complete the 30-second challenge.

How do streak and XP work?

Completing a daily challenge earns +25 XP once per calendar day, plus a +10 XP bonus for scoring 80 or higher. Your streak advances once per day and is saved locally in your browser.
